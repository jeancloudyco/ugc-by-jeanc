Questions

Before you book.

What usage rights are included?

Every video includes organic posting on your brand channels for 90 days. Running it as a paid ad, including Spark Ads, is an add-on. Longer terms are available on request.

Do you appear on camera?

Yes. I'm on camera for testimonials and day-in-the-life videos. Voiceover and screen-recorded demos are available too.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief and access to the product. For physical products, send a sample. For apps and software, a free or trial account works.

How do you handle disclosures?

Anything I post on my own accounts carries a clear paid-partnership label. I follow your guidelines for wording and placement.

What if I need changes?

One revision round is included with every video. Additional rounds are quoted before I start them.
